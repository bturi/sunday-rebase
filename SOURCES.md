## Rules of the road
- Press (Bloomberg, CNBC, FT) is not polled; it enters when a Tier 1 source relays it.
- X and Discord are not polled.
- A source with nothing new is listed as quiet in the colophon.
- Cap: at most 12 kept items from any single source per issue.
