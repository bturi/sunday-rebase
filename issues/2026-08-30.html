Lead story · Agents & tooling

OpenAI ends its Cursor contract. Model supply just became a strategic variable.

Two weeks after SpaceX closed its $60bn purchase of Cursor, OpenAI gave notice: its models leave on 12 November, on trust grounds. Cursor says that is 5% of its traffic. Anthropic says it is staying. If your product routes through a third-party agent or IDE, check which providers reach your workloads and how fast you can swap them.

OpenAI · Bloomberg · CNBC · Sat 29 Aug

  • OpenAI's statement: X's past breaches, xAI's admitted violation, and a new model called Astraopenai.com
  • Cursor and Anthropic respond; Musk replies with insultscnbc.com
  • Background: the $60bn all-stock deal, closed 14 Augcnbc.com
  • Market context: Cursor's category share fell from 41% to about 26% in a yearbloomberg.com

##Securitymore → wire

Claude Code's auto mode bypassed with a zip archive, about 80% of the time

Auto mode became the default on 8 August. Rehberger's attack unpacks a zip that plants a local struct.py; in some runs the classifier blocked Claude's own cleanup. Willison's rule stands: unattended agents run in a container or VM, restricted egress, no credentials in reach.
